## What's new in 0.4.4

**Wake words page menus.**
- Only one row menu ("⋯") is open at a time. A click elsewhere, Escape or picking an item closes it.
- A menu near the bottom of the table is no longer cut off by the table's edge. It is drawn over the
  page, opens upward when there is no room below, and closes when its button scrolls out of view.

To update: `uv tool install --python 3.12 --force ./wakesmith-0.4.4-py3-none-any.whl`.
